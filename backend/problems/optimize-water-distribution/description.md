There are n houses in a village, numbered 1 to n. For each house i, you can either build a well directly in that house at cost wells[i-1], or connect it to another house via a pipe. pipes[j] = [house1, house2, cost] represents a bidirectional pipe between house1 and house2 with the given cost. Return the minimum total cost to supply water to all houses (every house must end up with water, either from its own well or via a chain of pipes to a house that has a well).
